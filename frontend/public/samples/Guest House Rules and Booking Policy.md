# Guest House Rules and Booking Policy

These rules apply to every villa managed by VillaOps Bali. Please share them with every guest in the booking confirmation and again in the pre-arrival WhatsApp message.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM (Bali time, WITA). Early check-in from 10:00 AM and late check-out until 3:00 PM are free when the villa is not booked the same day; otherwise they cost 50% of one night. Late check-out after 3:00 PM is charged as a full night. Arrivals after 10:00 PM must tell us their flight number so the night staff can wait.

## Cancellation and refunds

- Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival: full refund.
- 7 to 13 days before arrival: 50% refund of the total booking value.
- Less than 7 days before arrival, or no-show: no refund.
- Peak season (20 December to 5 January, and the week of Nyepi) is non-refundable once confirmed, but the dates can be changed once, free of charge, if requested at least 30 days before arrival.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 working days. Stripe processing fees are not refunded. Date changes outside peak season are free if requested at least 7 days before arrival and the new dates are available.

## Deposits and payment

A 30% deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due 14 days before arrival. Bookings made less than 14 days before arrival are paid in full. A refundable security deposit of IDR 2,000,000 per villa is taken at check-in (card pre-authorisation or cash) and released within 3 days after check-out if there is no damage.

## Guests and visitors

The number of overnight guests may not exceed the villa's maximum shown on the listing. Children under 2 stay free and a baby cot is available on request. Day visitors are welcome until 10:00 PM, up to 4 at a time; parties, events and commercial photo or video shoots need written approval from management at least 7 days in advance.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. No amplified music outdoors at any time. On Nyepi (Balinese Day of Silence) all lights must be kept low and guests may not leave the villa grounds for 24 hours; our staff will deliver meals.

## Pool and safety

The pool is unsupervised. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. No glass near the pool. Pool cleaning takes place daily between 8:00 and 9:00 AM.

## Smoking, pets and damage

Smoking is allowed only in outdoor areas; smoking indoors results in a cleaning fee of IDR 1,500,000. Pets are not allowed, except certified assistance dogs with prior notice. Guests are responsible for damage beyond normal wear and tear; damage is deducted from the security deposit after the guest is shown photos.

## Housekeeping and amenities

Daily housekeeping is included between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Towels are changed every second day, bed linen every three days, or on request. Drinking water refills (galon) are free. Wi-Fi password is on the card by the TV; if the connection drops, guests should contact the villa host on WhatsApp.

## Emergencies

For any emergency call the villa host on WhatsApp first, then the 24/7 VillaOps duty line: +62 361 000 112. The nearest hospital and pharmacy are listed in the villa welcome book. For fire call 113, police 110, ambulance 118.
