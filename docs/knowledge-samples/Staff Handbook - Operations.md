# VillaOps Staff Handbook: Operations

This handbook covers the daily operating procedures for villa hosts, housekeeping and the front office team.

## Guest arrival procedure

1. Two days before arrival, the front office sends the pre-arrival WhatsApp message with the address pin, house rules, and an airport pickup offer.
2. On the arrival day, the villa host checks the villa against the readiness checklist by 12:00 PM: pool clean, AC working, welcome fruit and water, towels folded, Wi-Fi tested.
3. At check-in the host collects passport copies for foreign guests, takes the security deposit, and gives a 10-minute villa tour (pool rules, safe, emergency numbers).
4. The host logs the check-in in VillaOps the same day so the booking status changes to checked_in.

## Guest complaints and service recovery

Respond to every complaint within 15 minutes during working hours (7:00 AM to 10:00 PM) and within 1 hour at night. The villa host can approve compensation up to IDR 500,000 per stay (for example a free dinner, spa voucher or late check-out). Anything above that, or any request for a refund, must be escalated to the operations manager, who replies to the guest within 24 hours. Record every complaint and the action taken in the guest's notes in VillaOps.

## Maintenance and repairs

Urgent issues (no water, no power, AC broken in hot season, pool pump failure, security problem) must be fixed or have a technician on site within 2 hours. Non-urgent issues are scheduled within 3 days. If a villa can't be used, set its status to maintenance in VillaOps so no new bookings are taken, and tell the operations manager about any guest who must be moved.

## Housekeeping standards

Turnover cleaning between guests takes 3 hours for a 2-bedroom villa and 4 hours for 3 bedrooms or more. Every turnover includes: full linen change, bathroom deep clean, fridge emptied, pool skimmed, and a photo of each room uploaded to the shared drive for the owner. Lost and found items are kept for 90 days.

## Owner reporting

Owners receive a monthly statement by the 5th of each month showing occupancy, revenue, commission and expenses. Our management commission is 20% of room revenue. Repairs over IDR 3,000,000 need the owner's written approval before work starts, except emergencies.

## Data protection for staff

Staff must never send guest passport copies, phone numbers or booking details through personal WhatsApp or social media. Use only the VillaOps app and the company WhatsApp Business account. Lost staff phones must be reported to the operations manager within 1 hour so access can be revoked.
